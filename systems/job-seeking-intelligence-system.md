# Job-Seeking Intelligence System

**A personal labor-market intelligence and application operating system**

Status: Prototype / research system

## Purpose
Turn distributed labor-market signals into prioritized opportunities, customized application strategy, outreach, follow-up, and learning.

## Architecture
`MARKET SIGNALS → NORMALIZE → CORROBORATE → ENRICH → FIT / PRIORITY → HUMAN CUSTOMIZATION → APPLICATION → FOLLOW-UP → OUTCOME → FEEDBACK`

## Inputs
Job postings · employer career pages · ATS signals · professional networks · recruiter signals · referrals · labor-market data · user profile / portfolio

## Outputs
Opportunity intelligence · Job Intelligence Brief · Application strategy · Outreach queue · Follow-up queue · Market observations · Learning loop
