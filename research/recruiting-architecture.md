# Recruiting Architecture

## Thesis

Recruiting can be modeled as an information and workflow system connecting market demand, jobs, candidates, recruiters, communication, scheduling, ATS state, decisions, placements, and feedback.

## Core Flow

`JOB DEMAND → JOB INTELLIGENCE → TALENT SIGNALS → CANDIDATE INTELLIGENCE → HUMAN REVIEW → OUTREACH → CONVERSATION → SCREENING → ATS → INTERVIEW → DECISION → PLACEMENT → FEEDBACK`
