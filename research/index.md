# Research

Caphè research turns distributed signals into decision-ready intelligence.

## Core Research Programs

### Human-in-the-Loop Systems
How organizations design systems where machines collect, structure, enrich, monitor, and execute while humans retain authority over consequential interpretation and decisions.

### Recruiting Architecture
How recruiting organizations connect jobs, candidates, ATS data, communication, scheduling, intelligence, automation, and human decisions.

### Job-Seeking Intelligence Systems
How job seekers turn distributed labor-market signals into opportunity intelligence, application strategy, outreach, follow-up, and learning.

### Applied Learning & Information Workforce Systems
How applied learning systems prepare a workforce for information-intensive work — beginning with the High School Financial Literacy integration (2027).
