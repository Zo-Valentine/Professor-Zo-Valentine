# Job-Seeking Intelligence Systems

## Thesis

A job seeker can be modeled as an operator inside a labor-market information system rather than as a passive applicant.

## System

`MARKET SIGNALS → PERSONAL JOB INTELLIGENCE → OPPORTUNITY FILTER → FIT / PRIORITY → APPLICATION STRATEGY → HUMAN CUSTOMIZATION → APPLICATION / OUTREACH → FOLLOW-UP → RESPONSE → INTERVIEW → OUTCOME → LEARNING`

## Signal classes

- Employer career sites
- ATS / job boards
- LinkedIn
- Indeed
- Professional communities
- Referrals
- Recruiter conversations
- Public labor data
- Candidate observations

## Principle

Signal is not automatically an opportunity. Signals require corroboration, context, freshness, and human review.
