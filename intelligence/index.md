# Intelligence Systems

Caphè intelligence systems transform distributed signals into structured, evidence-backed information that supports human decisions and workflows.

## Intelligence Types

### Recruiting Intelligence
Jobs, candidates, recruiters, pipeline, labor-market signals.

### Job-Seeking Intelligence
Opportunities, labor-market signals, fit, application strategy, outcomes.

### Threat Intelligence
Distributed threat signals, entity relationships, patterns, investigation support.

### Property Intelligence
Properties, units, edge-scan attribution, leasing/disposition signals, workflow, human review.
Status: Prototype — see [Property Operations Intelligence Platform]({{ '/systems/property-operations-intelligence-platform/' | relative_url }}).

### Operational Intelligence
Business workflows, bottlenecks, capacity, exceptions, performance.

### Learning Intelligence
Learner activity, assessment, capability, intervention, outcomes.

## Universal Pattern
`SIGNALS → EVIDENCE → INTELLIGENCE → HUMAN DECISION → ACTION → FEEDBACK`
